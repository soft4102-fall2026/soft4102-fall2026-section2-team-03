PHASE 1:
  Findings, patterns and what this helps us understand:
  
    1. Individuals mainly discover events through Outlook, friends/faculty, and social media. Many Individuals are unaware of or do not use Navigate360.	Events are technically centralized, but Individuals do not use the existing source.	The main difficulty is not simply the lack of a central platform. Individuals need an event-discovery system that fits into how they already look for information and is easy to notice and use.
    
    2. Most Individuals have missed interesting events because they found out too late. Individuals also said they need information such as date, time, location, activities and purpose.	Individuals need timely and complete information to decide whether to attend.	Event discovery involves more than seeing that an event exists. Individuals need the right information at the right time, possibly through notifications, reminders, and clearer event details.
    
    3. Navigate360 provides a basic event list but has limited filtering, visuals, tags, attendance information, and other discovery features. Individuals also use social media and ask others when information is missing.	They have to put effort into finding relevant events and filling information gaps themselves.	The deeper difficulty is making event discovery relevant and convenient. They need better search, filtering, clear information, and potentially personalized recommendations so they can quickly find events that interest them.

  EMERGING UNDERSTANDING
    We now understand that: Individuals do not struggle only because university events are spread across different channels. A centralized event section already exists, but many Individuals are unaware of it or do not find it useful enough to use regularly. The deeper difficulty is helping Individuals discover relevant events easily, receive information early enough, and quickly understand whether an event is worth attending. This suggests that improving event discovery, personalization, event information, and timely notifications may be more important than simply creating another place to list events.


Phase 2:

Who? Students/Faculty/Event organizers

Need? Individuals want to easily discover relevant events, see important details, and avoid missing academic, social, and career opportunities.

Difficulty? Information is scattered across platforms, emails can be missed, and Navigate360 has limited filtering, details, and personalization, making relevant events harder to find.

Context? Individuals currently rely on Outlook, social media, flyers, friends, and Navigate360 to find events.

Problem Frame: University individuals need to easily discover relevant university events and access the information they need to decide whether to attend, but they often miss events or struggle to find useful information because events are promoted across multiple channels, information can be incomplete or difficult to find, and the existing event section is not regularly used.
