# Analysis (LM2)

Based on the execution record, interviews, tally sheet and visit chart in this folder. Criteria are in `01_protocol_and_changes`.

## 1. Observations (facts only)

**App pilot** (data as of Mon 2 Nov, 23:47, Qatar time)
- 8 students joined (S02–S09). S01 is the team's test code and is not counted.
- 8 of 8 opened the site on Days 1–2: 14 visits, 6 event pages opened, 7 filter or sort uses, 1 registration.
- Return days counted so far: 0 of 8. Returns only count from Day 3.

**Interviews** (8 students, 27 Oct – 2 Nov)

| Observation | Result |
|---|---|
| First heard about their last event from Instagram or friends | 6 of 8 (others: an instructor, campus booths; Outlook first: 0) |
| Look for events on their own | 5 of 8, mostly on the UDST Instagram |
| Never look, or only by chance | 3 of 8 |
| Have used Navigate360's events section | 2 of 8 (one checks it regularly) |
| Missed an event because they found out too late | 7 of 8 |

## 2. Comparison with predefined criteria

| Criterion (Lab 07, applied to 8 students) | Result |

| Failure: ≤ 30% (2 or fewer of 8) | — |

**Not comparable yet.** The pilot runs to Day 10 (10 Nov), and returns are counted on Days 3–10. We make no success or failure claim.

## 3. What the evidence supports

- **The problem is real:** 7 of 8 students missed an event because they found out too late.
- Most students hear about events through Instagram or friends, not through Outlook or Navigate360.

## 4. What the evidence challenges

- **A1 is only partly supported.** 5 of 8 students do look for events on their own, but mostly on Instagram, not on an events platform. Only 2 of 8 have used Navigate360's events section. Our site would have to compete with Instagram for that habit.

## 5. What remains uncertain

- Whether students return to **our** site without reminders. This is the main question, and the pilot hasn't finished.
- Whether the results apply to faculty (none replied).

## 6. Limitations

- Small sample (8), mostly people we know (about 2 of 8 were strangers), mostly IT and Business students.
- The screening question was not asked before the pilot links were sent.
- Mock events were listed alongside real ones.
- Some visits from the evening of 2 Nov were lost when the sheet was restored. This does not affect the return count, which starts on Day 3.
- The interview "self-checker" rule (50%+ supports A1) was set on 2 Nov, after the interviews, so it is exploratory and not a predefined criterion.

> We do not claim the venture is validated.
