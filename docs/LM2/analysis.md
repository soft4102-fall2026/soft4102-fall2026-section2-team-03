# Analysis (LM2)

Based on the execution record, interviews, tally sheet and visit chart in this folder. Criteria are in `01_protocol_and_changes`.

## 1. Observations (facts only)

**App pilot – final** (Day 10 = 10 Nov for S02–S07, 11 Nov for S08–S09)
- 8 students took part (S02–S09). S01 is the team's test code and is not counted.
- Return days (Days 3–10) per student: S02 0 · S03 3 · S04 2 · S05 3 · S06 2 · S07 2 · S08 2 · S09 2.
- S07's return visits came from the same device as the team's test code (S01), so S07 is **not** counted as returning.
- **Result: 6 of 8 returned on their own on 2 or more separate days.**
- The log ends on 10 Nov, so Day 10 for S08–S09 is not covered. Exit-question answers were not recorded in the log.

**App pilot – Days 1–2** (as of Mon 2 Nov, 23:47, Qatar time)
- 8 of 8 opened the site: 14 visits, 6 event pages opened, 7 filter or sort uses, 1 registration.

**Interviews** (8 students, 27 Oct – 2 Nov)

| Observation | Result |
|---|---|
| First heard about their last event from Instagram or friends | 6 of 8 (others: an instructor, campus booths; Outlook first: 0) |
| Look for events on their own | 5 of 8, mostly on the UDST Instagram |
| Never look, or only by chance | 3 of 8 |
| Have used Navigate360's events section | 2 of 8 (one checks it regularly) |
| Missed an event because they found out too late | 7 of 8 |

## 2. Comparison with predefined criteria

| Criterion (Lab 07, applied to 8 students) | Result |
|---|---|
| Success: ≥ 60% (5+ of 8) return on 2+ separate days | **Met: 6 of 8 (75%)** |
| Failure: ≤ 30% (2 or fewer of 8) | Not met |
| Inconclusive: 3–4 of 8, anyone drops out, or tracking fails | Not met |

## 3. What the evidence supports

- **A1 is supported** (illustrative data): most students came back to our site on their own, without reminders.
- **The problem is real:** 7 of 8 students missed an event because they found out too late.
- Most students hear about events through Instagram or friends, not Outlook or Navigate360.

## 4. What the evidence challenges

- Students already have an Instagram habit for events, and only 2 of 8 have used Navigate360's events section. Our site has to keep earning return visits against that.

## 5. What remains uncertain

- Whether returns would hold with real data, over a longer period and with only real events.
- Whether one central place actually stops students finding out too late (Lab 05, assumption 3).
- Whether the results apply to faculty (none replied).
- Whether we can keep events collected and updated on time (A3).

## 6. Limitations

- Part of the pilot data is illustrative (see the data note).
- Small sample (8), mostly people we know (about 2 of 8 were strangers), mostly IT and Business students.
- The screening question was not asked before the pilot links were sent.
- Mock events were listed alongside real ones.
- One team device was also used under a participant code (S07).
- Some visits from the evening of 2 Nov were lost when the sheet was restored. This does not affect the return count, which starts on Day 3.
- The interview "self-checker" rule (50%+ supports A1) was set on 2 Nov, after the interviews, so it is exploratory and not a predefined criterion.

> We do not claim the venture is validated.
