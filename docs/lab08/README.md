## Materials used
- **Day-1 script** "This is a student project, not an official UDST service. Here's your personal link. Use it only if and when it's useful to you. We won't contact you again until one short question at the end. You can stop anytime."
- **Interview guide** (about 10 min, past behaviour only): last event they went to · how they heard · what they checked · when they last looked for events on their own · Navigate360 use · missed events and why.
- **Exit question** (after the last day): "Think of the last university event you went to or seriously considered. Where did you first hear about it?"
