Opportunity Snapshot

Starting Statement: We are interested in investigating how students discover and decide to attend university events because most students have  missed events we cared about when the only announcement channel was from different sources 

What's happening is that event announcements go out mostly through Outlook, occasional flyers and club social media, so they compete with academic emails and get lost or missed.This happens all semester, but it gets worse during busy email periods like the start of term or exam weeks.This matters because students miss out on career, social, and academic opportunities, and organizers' planning effort goes to waste.
The people affected are mainly students, but also organizers (clubs, departments, faculty) and the university if engagement matters to them.

Right now it's handled through mass emails, occasional flyers, and scattered club social media. There is also a small events section in navigate but it's missing a few important features.
